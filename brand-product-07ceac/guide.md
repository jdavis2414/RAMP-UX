## Color roles

- **Background** (`#ffffff`) — background: page canvas
- **Foreground** (`#111111`) — foreground: body text and headings
- **Accent** (`#1677ff`) — accent: primary actions and emphasis
- **Surface** (`#f7f8fa`) — surface: cards and panels
- **Muted** (`#6b7280`) — muted: secondary text and metadata
- **Border** (`#d9dee7`) — border: rules and dividers
